How It Works

The decentralized architecture workflow powered by Ethereum smart contracts and MetaMask.

Step 1

Connect Wallet

Voters connect their MetaMask wallet (Ethereum Web3 provider) to authenticate their unique public address.

Step 2

Review Candidates

Voters examine the certified candidates, their policy agendas, and unique candidate identifier numbers.

Step 3

Sign & Cast Ballot

Voters trigger the Solidity function vote(candidateId). MetaMask signs the transaction with gas fees.

Step 4

Verify on Ledger

The transaction is mined into a block on Ganache. The contract enforces single-vote eligibility and updates the ledger.

Security & Blockchain Architecture

Key cryptographic principles behind a blockchain-powered electronic voting system.

🔒

Immutability

Once a vote is confirmed on the blockchain, cryptographic hashing ensures that neither administrators nor attackers can alter or delete the record.

⚖️

Smart Contract Enforcement

Voting rules, candidate lists, and election deadlines are executed automatically via Solidity code without human intervention.

🚫

Double-Voting Prevention

The contract maintains a mapping(address => Voter) public voters, instantaneously reverting any subsequent attempt from the same address.

👁️

Public Auditability

Every transaction hash and event log can be independently inspected by voters, candidates, and third-party auditors using blockchain explorers.
